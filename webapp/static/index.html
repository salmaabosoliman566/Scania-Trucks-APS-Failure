Air Pressure System (APS) in Scania Trucks

Heavy transport vehicles depend on pressurized air to execute safe braking, swift pneumatic gear transitions, and adaptive chassis suspension leveling. The Scania APS system comprises compressors, multi-circuit protection valves, desiccant air dryers, and air reservoirs.

A failure in an APS component jeopardizes driver safety, immobilizes the vehicle, and incurs heavy towing and cargo penalty costs ($500 per breakdown). By processing operational telemetry collected by vehicle ECU nodes, machine learning identifies degradation patterns before breakdown occurs.

Challenge Cost Optimization Formula

The IDA 2016 challenge establishes an asymmetric loss function:

Total_Cost = (Cost_1 × False_Positives) + (Cost_2 × False_Negatives)
Total_Cost = (10 × FP) + (500 × FN)

Our model balances recall and precision, intentionally prioritizing high recall (75.0%) to minimize costly false negatives while keeping false positives controlled.
